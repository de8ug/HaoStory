# Story shapes

Choose one shape per output. Do not combine an entire project history into one short video.

## Changed judgment

1. We believed X.
2. Evidence contradicted it.
3. We changed Y.
4. The result was Z.

## Failure to proof

1. Show the failure immediately.
2. Explain why the obvious fix did not work.
3. Show the decisive change.
4. Prove the result with a test or before/after image.

## Constraint-led design

1. State the constraint.
2. Show the tempting but unsustainable path.
3. Explain the narrower choice.
4. Show why the narrower system is more durable.

## Video pacing

- 0–2s: surprising result or conflict;
- 2–8s: intent and stakes;
- 8–20s: failed assumption or constraint;
- 20–35s: decision and implementation;
- 35–50s: proof;
- 50–60s: changed judgment and next experiment.
