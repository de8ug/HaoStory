# UP2U · HaoStory experiment 001

This is the first public production experiment for HaoStory.

UP2U began as an AI navigation site. During product discussion, its position became narrower and more credible: a personally curated navigator of AI problems, ideas, projects, and real-world experiments. HaoStory captured the positioning decision, the working homepage, and the successful build/test output while the work happened.
